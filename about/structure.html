Group structure

The Combine is a holding company. It holds controlling stakes in its two main subsidiaries, which are joint-stock companies, and manages the satellite mines and the by-products plant through them.

  • Iradi Antimony and Rare Metals Combine (head office, Talas)
    • Sary-Bulak Antimony Works (SBAW): Sary-Bulak mine and flotation mill
      • Kemer Antimony Mine
      • Kyzyl-Jar Antimony Shafts
    • Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex: smelter and trioxide shop
      • Chatkal Antimony Processing Mill
      • Chatkal Rare Earth Facility (by-products)

The satellite mines are listed under the Sary-Bulak Antimony Works for management purposes. Their ore is concentrated at the Chatkal Antimony Processing Mill.
