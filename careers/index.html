Working at the Combine

Careers

  • Working at the Combine

The Combine employs about 4,700 people at Sary-Bulak, Chatkal and the head office in Talas. We recruit miners, engineers, metallurgists, chemists, electricians, drivers and office staff.

What we offer

  • Wages paid partly in US dollars
  • Company buses from Talas and the villages of the Talas valley
  • Company housing at Rudnik and Chatkal
  • Annual medical examination at the company polyclinics
  • Training at the Chatkal technical lyceum

Vacancies

Position Location Requirements
Mining engineer Sary-Bulak Higher education in mining; underground experience
Underground electrician Sary-Bulak Electrical qualification; underground experience preferred
Flotation operator Chatkal Technical education; shift work
Laboratory chemist Chatkal Higher education in chemistry
Heavy truck driver Kemer and Kyzyl-Jar Category C licence

How to apply

Send your CV to the Human Resources Department: hr@iarmc.com, tel. +996-39-6-21-40-30, or apply in person at the head office, 14 Metallurgy Avenue, Talas.
