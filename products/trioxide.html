Antimony trioxide

Products

  • Antimony metal
  • Antimony trioxide
  • Antimony concentrate
  • Bismuth and tellurium
  • Sales and markets

The Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex produces high-purity antimony trioxide by re-volatilising refined antimony metal under controlled conditions. Antimony trioxide is used mainly as a flame-retardant synergist in plastics and textiles, and in glass, including glass for solar panels.

Specification

Typical specification
Parameter Value
Sb2O3, % not less than 99.50
As2O3, % not more than 0.060
PbO, % not more than 0.100
Fe2O3, % not more than 0.005
Whiteness, % not less than 95
Mean particle size, micrometres 0.8 to 1.5

Each lot is supplied with a certificate of analysis from the Chatkal laboratory.

Packing

Paper bags of 25 kg on pallets, or bulk bags of 1 t.

Item Details
Annual output 3,100 t (2025)
Shipment By rail from the Chatkal siding, or by truck with Trans-Chuy Freight & Logistics
