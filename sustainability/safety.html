Health and safety

Sustainability

  • Environment
  • Health and safety
  • Community
  • Environmental reports

Underground mining and smelting carry serious risks. The Combine's first duty is the safety of its labour collective.

Safety indicators
Indicator 2023 2024 2025
Fatal accidents 1 2 0
Lost-time injuries 41 37 33
Lost-time injury frequency rate (per 1 million hours worked) 3.1 2.8 2.6

Mine rescue

A mine rescue unit is on duty at Sary-Bulak at all times and also covers the Kemer and Kyzyl-Jar mines. Mine rescue teams take part in training exercises every quarter.

Health

Underground workers and smelter workers have a medical examination every year at the polyclinics of Rudnik and the Chatkal settlement, including a chest X-ray. Dust suppression is used at the crushing plants and underground.
