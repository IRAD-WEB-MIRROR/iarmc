Grievance procedure

Anyone who lives near the Combine's operations or uses the roads, water or pastures around them can raise a concern with the Combine. Concerns are recorded and answered, and the Combine does not charge for this.

How to raise a concern

WayDetails
In personCommunity office, house of culture, Rudnik; community office, Chatkal settlement. Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00
By telephoneCommunity office Rudnik +996-39-7-31-2-18; community office Chatkal +996-39-7-34-1-07
In writingLetter to the Community Relations Department, 14 Metallurgy Avenue, Talas, or a letter box at either community office
Through the districtThrough the village head or a member of the Sary-Bulak District council, who passes the concern to the community office

What happens next

  1. The concern is registered and given a number. You will receive the number within five working days.
  2. The relevant department investigates. For dust, water or a dam, the Department of Ecology and Industrial Safety visits the site.
  3. You receive a written answer within 30 days. If you do not agree with it, you can ask for a meeting with the director of the site.
  4. You can also take a concern to the Chatkal Placer Mining Inspectorate, the Sary-Bulak District administration or the courts at any time.
