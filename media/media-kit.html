The Combine in brief

ItemDetails
Full nameIradi Antimony and Rare Metals Combine, closed joint-stock company (IARMC CJSC)
Founded1994, on the basis of the Iradi Antimony Combine (1961)
Head office14 Metallurgy Avenue, Talas
General DirectorAlmaz Toktosunov
OperationsSary-Bulak Antimony Works (mine and mill); Kemer and Kyzyl-Jar mines; Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex; Kara-Suu Hydroelectric Station
Antimony mined, 202515,100 t contained
Employeesabout 4,700
ProductsAntimony metal, antimony trioxide, antimony concentrate, bismuth, tellurium, sulphuric acid

Logo

The Combine's logo is the symbol of antimony, Sb, with its atomic number, 51, on a navy tile, beside the name IARMC. It may be used in reports about the Combine without changes to its colours or proportions.
