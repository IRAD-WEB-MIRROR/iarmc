News archive, 1994–2020

From the Combine's press releases before the new website. Full texts on request from the Press Service.

  • 16-12-2020 Production results for 2020
    The Combine worked through the year without stopping production. Results will be published in the Annual Report 2020.
  • 03-04-2020 Work during the quarantine
    Shifts at Sary-Bulak and Chatkal continue with temperature checks at the shaft heads and the furnace halls. Company buses run with half the seats filled.
  • 02-09-2019 Upper-gorge tailings cell dropped
    After meetings with the herders of the Sary-Bulak valley, the Combine will not build the planned upper-gorge cell of the tailings facility.
  • 19-04-2019 Break in the tailings pipeline at Sary-Bulak
    A section of the tailings pipeline broke on 18 April. The pipeline was stopped and the spill contained; clean-up of the Sary-Bulak stream is under way with the Chatkal Placer Mining Inspectorate.
  • 14-11-2018 Sulphuric acid from the furnace gases
    The new gas-capture unit at Chatkal has started producing sulphuric acid for sale in the ISEZ.
  • 07-10-2016 SO2 scrubbers at Chatkal
    The scrubbers and electrostatic precipitators designed under Chief Engineer Artem Kozlov have been commissioned on the working furnaces.
  • 17-02-2013 Miners' memorial unveiled
    A memorial to the miners who died at Sary-Bulak was unveiled by the Shaft No. 1 headframe on the first anniversary of the rockfall at Shaft No. 2.
  • 17-02-2012 Rockfall at Shaft No. 2
    Four miners died in a rockfall in the old stopes of Shaft No. 2. The Combine expresses its deepest condolences to the families. Work in the old stopes is stopped until the inquiry is complete.
  • 01-06-2011 Kyzyl-Jar shafts reopen
    Work at the two Kyzyl-Jar shafts resumes after four years.
  • 30-09-2009 Government acquires a minority stake
    The ISEZ Government has acquired a minority stake in the Combine.
  • 12-11-2007 Production resumes
    Production at Sary-Bulak and Chatkal resumes as the rail links are restored. The Kyzyl-Jar shafts remain closed.
  • 14-09-2001 The Central incline
    Work begins on the Central incline at Sary-Bulak, to reach the lower levels without the old shafts.
  • 06-07-1998 Agreement with the miners' union
    The strike at Sary-Bulak has ended. Under the agreement, part of every wage will be paid in US dollars.
  • 20-10-1997 Kemer mine reopens
    Mining resumes at Kemer after the levels were pumped out and re-equipped.
  • 18-11-1994 Charter of the Combine
    The Sary-Bulak Antimony Works and the Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex become joint-stock companies under the new Combine. Employees of the Sary-Bulak works receive a minority of its shares.
